## 3 big illustrated headlines

1. **COP31 COLLAPSES — NO DEAL** — The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey,
   ends with no overarching agreement: talks ended Sun 22 Nov and Monday's
   wires led with the fallout, after the EU and ~130 parties rejected a
   text that never mentions fossil fuels. Longest COP ever; first breakdown
   since Copenhagen 2009. (CNN, Reuters, Le Monde, WSJ — reported Mon 23 Nov;
   the final plenary collapsed Sun 22 Nov, flagged in the item itself.)
   Region: Europe.
2. **24 DEAD IN JAVA CANDY-FACTORY FIRE** — Indonesia confirms 24 dead and
   about 30 still missing after a fire tore through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia
   confectionery plant in Cikande, Banten, on Saturday evening; ~300 workers
   were on shift. Cause suspected to be an air-conditioner short circuit.
   (Reuters, 23 Nov.) Region: Asia.
3. **AI SELL-OFF RATTLES WORLD MARKETS** — Fears the AI boom is overpriced
   drive markets down: Japan's Nikkei −5.06%, Hong Kong to a 19-month low,
   S&P 500 −3.7% last week; Wall Street futures bounce Monday. (One Turn,
   Reuters, 23 Nov.) Region: Global.
